## PRODUCT

Žar Klik is a portable food-heating container designed primarily for professional truck drivers and people who spend long periods travelling or working without access to a kitchen.

It allows users to heat a meal they have brought from home without electricity or an open flame.

The system uses:

1. A controlled chemical reaction between CaO and water
2. Heat transfer to an aluminium food container
3. Even heating of the meal

Main brand message:

“Zagrejan obrok bilo kad i bilo gde.”

The product should feel:

- practical
- reliable
- safe
- warm and human
- designed for travel
- simple to use
- modern, but not overly futuristic

- mobile-first
